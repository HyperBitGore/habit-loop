Habit Loop

Build habits that stick. Plan days that work.

Habit Loop is a simple, free habit tracker and daily todo planner. Check off habits on the schedule you choose, plan todos day by day, and watch your consistency grow on a history calendar — without clutter, gimmicks, or a mobile app to install.

Log in or create an account

What you can do with Habit Loop

  • Track habits on your schedule. Repeat a habit every N days or only on the weekdays you pick. Complete, skip, or edit a habit in one tap.
  • Plan todos by date. Each day gets its own list, with quick navigation between Yesterday, Today, and Tomorrow, and the option to defer a todo to the next day.
  • See your history. A per-habit calendar shows completed, skipped, scheduled, and untargeted days so you can spot streaks and gaps at a glance.
  • Measure what matters. Attach optional numeric metrics to habits — minutes read, kilometers run, pages written — and record a value for any date.
  • Work toward goals. Create goals with ordered steps and link individual steps to the todos that move them forward.
  • Keep notes. Add dated notes to any habit or todo to remember context, blockers, or wins.
  • Bring your data with you. Import from a uHabit database, and export your habits to uHabit or CSV at any time. Your data is yours.

How it works

  • 1. Create an account. Registration takes a minute and uses email verification to keep accounts secure.
  • 2. Add your habits and todos. Set each habit's schedule and start checking things off.
  • 3. Review your progress. Use the history calendar and habit summaries to keep your streaks alive.

Frequently asked questions

Is Habit Loop free?
Yes. Habit Loop is free to use and supported by advertising.

Do I need to install anything?
No. Habit Loop runs in your browser on desktop and mobile.

Can I get my data out?
Yes. You can export your habits and history to CSV or a uHabit database from your profile at any time, and you can delete your account and its data whenever you choose.

How is my account protected?
Accounts use email verification, hashed passwords, server-side sessions, and bot protection on login and registration.
