Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Individual Developer ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use Habit Loop at todosloop.com (the "Service").

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Account information: your username, email address, and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form rather than as plain text.
  • Content you create: habits, todos, schedules, completion history, profile changes, and related account data.
  • Authentication and security information: session tokens, email-verification and password-reset tokens, request details, and information used to prevent abuse.
  • Device and network information: information such as IP address, browser, and request timestamps that may be recorded in server, security, or provider logs.
  • Advertising information: Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure advertising through Google AdSense.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide, maintain, and secure the Service;
  • authenticate accounts and send verification or password-reset email;
  • store and display your habits, todos, and account settings;
  • detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

Service providers

We use carefully selected third-party providers to operate parts of the Service:

  • Resend: sends account verification and password-reset email. Your email address and message details may be processed by Resend to deliver those messages.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: verifies that registration, login, and password-reset requests are made by people rather than automated systems. Cloudflare may process request and device information for this purpose.
  • Google AdSense: displays and measures advertising. Google may use cookies and similar technologies as described in Google's advertising policies.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to legal process, protect the Service or other people, or investigate suspected misuse.

Cookies and similar technologies

Habit Loop uses a necessary authentication cookie to keep you signed in. Google AdSense may set advertising cookies or use similar technologies. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling necessary cookies may prevent account features from working. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the Service does not load its AdSense advertising script. You can also manage Google's advertising personalization settings through your Google account or Google's ad settings.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account and content information while your account remains active and for as long as reasonably necessary for security, backup, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. You can delete your account from the User settings page, or request access, correction, or deletion by emailing privacy@todosloop.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict some processing. You may also opt out of personalized advertising through available browser, device, or Google controls. Contact us at privacy@todosloop.com to exercise applicable rights.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date. Continued use of the Service after an update means the updated policy applies to your use of the Service.

Contact
